**Fig. 1 | Sparse optogenetic excitation fails to initiate rhythmic motor activity unless the balanced spinal network is near threshold, where subset-to-subset variability is governed by multi-synaptic recurrent amplification.**

**a**, Top: balanced spinal motor network model (Lindén et al., 2022; $N = 200$ neurons: 100 excitatory [E], 100 inhibitory [I]; pairwise connection probability $C = 0.1$; inhibitory-to-excitatory weight ratio $g = 1$; nominal spectral radius $W_{\mathrm{radius}} = 1.0$) receiving uniform global tonic drive $D$ (0–20) to all neurons and a 5 s photostimulation pulse (473 nm laser, 1–6 s) targeted to a random 10% of E cells ($n = 10$). Bottom: normalized channelrhodopsin-2 (ChR2) photocurrents ($I / I_{\max}$) comparing a non-desensitizing step ($I_{\mathrm{ss}}/I_{\mathrm{peak}} = 1.0$, dashed teal), uniform desensitizing ChR2 ($I_{\mathrm{ss}}/I_{\mathrm{peak}} = 0.3$, $\tau_{\mathrm{des}} = 40$ ms, $\tau_{\mathrm{off}} = 15$ ms, violet), and spatially heterogeneous illumination (per-cell $\varphi \sim U(0, 1)$) with intensity-dependent desensitization ($I_{\mathrm{ss}}/I_{\mathrm{peak}}$ scaling from 0.9 to 0.3, $\tau_{\mathrm{des}}$ from 150 to 40 ms, half-saturation $K = 0.25$; shown for $\varphi = 0.75$ and $0.30$, crimson).

**b–d**, Representative phase-sorted population firing-rate rasters (top, in sp/s; E and I separated by horizontal white line) and antagonist flexor/extensor motor-nerve readouts (bottom; opposite-phase pools at $\pm\pi/2$, spread $\pi/8$). Red arrowheads indicate the 10 opsin E cells shaded by $\varphi$; blue bars and dashed lines denote the 1–6 s laser epoch (variable light, $I_{\max} = 80$). **b**, From rest ($D = 0$), laser stimulation drives tonic firing in the illuminated E cells but fails to induce rotational population waves or rhythmic nerve bursts (unlit temporal rate s.d. $= 0.21$ sp/s, leading Jacobian eigenvalue $\mathrm{Re}\,\lambda_{\max} = 0.53$, $f = \text{none}$). **c**, With subthreshold background tonic drive near the intrinsic bifurcation ($D = 12$; pre-laser s.d. $= 0.52$ sp/s, post-laser s.d. $= 1.14$ sp/s), the same 10% E photostimulation gates full-population rotational firing and alternating nerve bursts during light delivery (s.d. $= 5.35$ sp/s, $\mathrm{Re}\,\lambda_{\max} = 1.05$, peak temporal frequency $f = 0.66$ Hz). **d**, Suprathreshold global drive ($D = 20$, no laser) generates sustained intrinsic rhythmicity throughout the trial (s.d. $= 8.60$ sp/s, $\mathrm{Re}\,\lambda_{\max} = 1.12$, $f = 0.62$ Hz).

**e**, Unlit oscillation amplitude (mean temporal firing-rate s.d. across the 190 unlit neurons during 2–6 s, in sp/s; $n = 30$ random 10% E draws per tile) at rest ($D = 0$) across peak photocurrent intensities ($I_{\max} = 10\text{–}320$) and ChR2 desensitization regimes (uniform $I_{\mathrm{ss}}/I_{\mathrm{peak}} = 0.1\text{–}1.0$ and variable-light `var. φ`). No combination evokes rhythmicity (all 36 tiles $\le 0.43$ sp/s vs. 0.15 sp/s without light and $8.69 \pm 0.27$ sp/s at $D = 20$; max $\mathrm{Re}\,\lambda_{\max} = 0.79 < 1$). Illuminating a random 10% of all neurons (20/200 mixed E/I cells, $I_{\max} = 80$) at $D = 0$ similarly fails ($0.20 \pm 0.05$ sp/s desensitizing; $0.24 \pm 0.07$ sp/s step).

**f**, Phase diagram of unlit oscillation amplitude (rate s.d., sp/s) across background tonic drive $D$ (0–20) and peak intensity $I_{\max}$ (5–320; leftmost column `Off` shows matched no-laser controls, $n = 30$; laser tiles, $n = 20$) for intensity-dependent desensitizing ChR2 (`var. light φ`), on the shared colour scale of **e**. Dotted cyan line indicates the intrinsic bifurcation threshold without light ($D_{\mathrm{th}} \approx 13.5$); dashed white contour marks rhythmicity onset ($\mathrm{s.d.} = 2.0$ sp/s). Photostimulation fails for all $D \le 8$ regardless of intensity, gates rhythms only near $D_{\mathrm{th}}$ ($D \approx 10\text{–}13$) at moderate plateau currents, and suppresses intrinsic suprathreshold rhythms at excessive sustained currents ($I_{\max} \ge 160$) by pinning opsin cells near saturation.

**g, h**, Unlit oscillation amplitude (rate s.d., sp/s; **g**) and linearized $\mathrm{Re}\,\lambda_{\max}$ of $\mathrm{diag}(f'(\bar{V}))W$ (**h**) versus background tonic drive $D$ (mean $\pm$ s.d.; $n = 30$ draws in **g**, $n = 15$ in **h**) comparing variable-light desensitizing stimulation ($I_{\max} = 40$, red) against matched no-laser controls (charcoal). Pink shading highlights the near-threshold window ($D = 10\text{–}13.5$). For $D \le 10$, 0% of draws evoke rhythms ($\mathrm{s.d.} \ge 2$ sp/s and $\ge 2\times$ baseline) and $\mathrm{Re}\,\lambda_{\max} < 1$. Near threshold, variable-light desensitizing stimulation lifts $\mathrm{Re}\,\lambda_{\max}$ to or above 1 and evokes rhythms in 23% ($D = 11$), 50% ($D = 12$; $2.22 \pm 1.28$ vs. $0.72 \pm 0.05$ sp/s no laser), and 60% ($D = 13$; $3.01 \pm 1.64$ vs. $1.20 \pm 0.16$ sp/s) of draws. Results replicate across $n = 10$ independent random network realizations (15 draws each, $I_{\max} = 40$: $D = 0$ laser $0.17 \pm 0.00$ vs. $0.15 \pm 0.00$ sp/s no laser; $D = 12$ laser $1.92 \pm 0.48$ vs. $0.89 \pm 0.26$ sp/s; $D = 20$ reference $7.27 \pm 3.02$ sp/s).

**i**, Complex eigenvalue spectra of $\mathrm{diag}(f'(\bar{V}))W$ during 2–6 s for the representative draw in **b–d** (open rings mark leading conjugate pairs). At $D = 0$, sparse E stimulation leaves unlit E and I neurons on the low-gain foot of the sigmoidal transfer function $f(V)$, trapping the entire spectrum inside $\mathrm{Re}\,\lambda < 1$ (dashed line); only when background drive already places the network near threshold does 10% E stimulation push the oscillatory conjugate pair across $\mathrm{Re}\,\lambda = 1$.

**j**, Repeated random shuffling of which 10 excitatory cells receive illumination ($n = 500$ independent subsets per condition; horizontal black ticks show median, vertical bars interquartile range; percentages denote draws with rate $\mathrm{s.d.} \ge 2$ sp/s). At rest ($D = 0$), shuffling yields virtually zero variability and universal failure (variable light $I_{\max} = 40$: $0.17 \pm 0.01$ sp/s, range $0.16\text{–}0.20$ sp/s, 0/500 $\ge 2$ sp/s, max $\mathrm{Re}\,\lambda_{\max} = 0.68$; uniform $I_{\max} = 80$: $0.34 \pm 0.15$ sp/s, median $0.31$ sp/s, 1/500 $\ge 2$ sp/s). Near threshold ($D = 12$, $I_{\max} = 40$), shuffling produces nearly 20-fold variability (variable light: $2.06 \pm 1.51$ sp/s, range $0.49\text{–}8.41$ sp/s, 36.2% $\ge 2$ sp/s; uniform light: $2.27 \pm 1.78$ sp/s, range $0.49\text{–}9.55$ sp/s, 40.4% $\ge 2$ sp/s). Variance decomposition across 20 fixed 10-cell subsets repeated over 25 independent noise realizations each ($D = 12$, uniform $I_{\max} = 40$) confirms that 99.4% of total variance reflects deterministic cell-subset identity (subset means $0.98\text{–}10.05$ sp/s) and only 0.6% reflects trial-to-trial membrane noise (mean within-subset s.d. $= 0.17$ sp/s).

**k**, Analytical zero-simulation prediction of the 500-shuffle outcomes at $D = 12$ (variable light, $I_{\max} = 40$) from the connectivity matrix $W$ alone. The steady-state linear-response voltage shift $V_{\mathrm{lin}} = D + (I - f'(D)W)^{-1} I_{\mathrm{ss}}$ is used to evaluate the predicted leading eigenvalue $\mathrm{Re}\,\lambda_{\max}^{\mathrm{pred}}$ of $\mathrm{diag}(f'(V_{\mathrm{lin}}))W$ (x-axis) and the net multi-synaptic unlit depolarization $\langle\Delta V\rangle$ (colour scale). Predicted $\mathrm{Re}\,\lambda_{\max}^{\mathrm{pred}}$ tightly tracks simulated oscillation amplitude (Spearman $\rho = 0.84$, Pearson $r = 0.77$, $n = 500$), exhibiting a sharp transition across $\mathrm{Re}\,\lambda_{\max}^{\mathrm{pred}} = 1$. Individual E neurons span net inhibitory to strongly excitatory multi-synaptic population impact ($g_{\mathrm{eff}} = -0.070$ to $+0.126$ per unit current); causally selecting the top-10 versus bottom-10 E cells by analytical $g_{\mathrm{eff}}$ alone (red and blue diamonds, uniform $I_{\max} = 40$) produces an almost 10-fold separation at $D = 12$ ($3.76$ sp/s, $\mathrm{Re}\,\lambda_{\max} = 1.02$ vs. $0.39$ sp/s, $\mathrm{Re}\,\lambda_{\max} = 0.75$) while still failing to evoke rhythms at $D = 0$ ($0.43$ sp/s, $\mathrm{Re}\,\lambda_{\max} = 0.88$).

**l**, Variance in 500-shuffle $D = 12$ oscillation amplitude explained ($R^2$; $|\text{Spearman }\rho|$ in parentheses) by candidate predictors. Multi-synaptic linear-response stability ($\mathrm{Re}\,\lambda_{\max}^{\mathrm{pred}}$: $R^2 = 0.59$, $\rho = 0.84$; uniform-light shuffles: $R^2 = 0.54$, $\rho = 0.80$) and net multi-synaptic depolarization ($\langle\Delta V\rangle$: $R^2 = 0.48$, $\rho = 0.75$) account for most subset-to-subset variability, whereas direct 1-hop E-minus-I out-degree ($R^2 = 0.08$, $\rho = 0.31$), total steady photocurrent ($R^2 = 0.01$), dominant eigenmode amplitude $|v_i|$ ($R^2 < 0.001$), and oscillatory phase coherence $R$ ($R^2 < 0.001$) have little or no explanatory power.
